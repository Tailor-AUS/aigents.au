## 🛠️ Days 2–3: The 48-Hour AI Workflow Crash Course

When the student is selected, they spend 48 hours mastering the three specific AI workflows pitched on `[company].aigents.au`:

### Module 1: The Telemetry & Sensor Data Pipeline (Python / Polars)
* **Goal:** Teach the student how to parse 500k+ rows of industrial sensor logs in seconds.
* **The Stack:** Python, Polars, DuckDB, Jupyter / Cursor.
* **The Drill:** Provide a messy industrial CSV with missing values and temperature spikes. Student writes a 20-line script with an LLM agent to clean the data, calculate rolling averages, and flag anomalies automatically.
* **Talking Point in Interview:** *"In past projects, I built an automated Polars pipeline that reduced telemetry diagnostic time by 80% without manual spreadsheet wrangling."*

### Module 2: The Sovereign Standards RAG System
* **Goal:** Teach the student how to query Australian & ISO standards using vector retrieval.
* **The Stack:** Local vector embeddings (e.g. Chroma/pgvector) over AS/NZS electrical/mining standards.
* **The Drill:** Student asks: *"What are the mandatory thermal relief valve sizing requirements under AS 1210?"* and gets the exact clause, citation, and formula in 3 seconds.
* **Talking Point in Interview:** *"Rather than losing 4 hours digging through regulatory PDFs, I use a vectorised RAG harness to cross-reference compliance clauses instantly with full citation trails."*

### Module 3: Operational PMO & Shift Handoff Synthesis
* **Goal:** Teach the student how to turn meeting voice memos into structured engineering change logs and FMEA risk registers.
* **The Stack:** Whisper transcription + structured JSON prompt extraction.
* **Talking Point in Interview:** *"I automate meeting and shift handoff synthesis into Jira action items, so your senior leads don't lose hours to administrative overhead."*

## 🎯 Day 4: Mock Technical Interview Rehearsal

### The 3 Rules of the Interview
1. **Never say "AI will replace engineers":** Frame yourself as the **operational force-multiplier**. You do the data wrangling and compliance digging so the senior engineers can design.
2. **Bring working code / demos:** When asked about problem solving, share screen and show the Python telemetry script or the standards search tool.
3. **Emphasize First-Principles Verification:** Remind the panel: *"AI generates the boilerplate and parses the logs, but my engineering background (ATAR 99) is how I mathematically verify that the output is physically sound."*

### Standard Panel Questions & Scripted Responses

#### Q1: "You're an undergraduate. Why shouldn't we hire someone with 3 years of industry experience?"
> **The Script:**  
> *"An experienced engineer will cost you $150k+ and will spend half their time bogged down in routine data wrangling and documentation. Traditional interns take 3 months of senior mentoring just to configure their environment.  
> I come equipped with modern AI workflow harnesses. On Day 1, I can ingest your sensor logs, cross-reference your compliance specs, and automate your testing loops without hand-holding. You get senior-grade operational velocity at student placement economics."*

#### Q2: "How do you ensure AI outputs aren't hallucinating in safety-critical mining/energy systems?"
> **The Script:**  
> *"AI is never used for unverified final design. I treat AI strictly as a high-speed ingestion and syntax tool. Every standards citation is bound to an exact clause reference, and every telemetry anomaly model is constrained by first-principles thermodynamic and electrical boundary conditions that I verify manually."*
